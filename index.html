A CLEAR VIEW OF THE SIMULATION

About the sim

VentureQuest is a learning prototype for testing startup assumptions. Its scores and market activity are illustrative prompts, not predictions or evidence of business performance.

Scores and scenarios

The feasibility score comes from Ollama locally when available; otherwise a lightweight heuristic is used. Market indices and scenario events use fictional game math, not live market or customer data.

Signals and leaderboard

Community signal points, impact badges, map dots, and rankings are calculated from ventures in this browser. They are not money, verified impact, real locations, or a shared global leaderboard.

AI and your draft

On your computer, optional Ollama processes the idea locally. The hosted demo uses a local fallback coach. AI drafts can be wrong and should be checked with users; they are not investment advice.

Payments and storage

The main demo moves no money. Optional PayPal features use Sandbox only when configured. Venture drafts and signals are stored in this browser and are not shared across visitors.
